# Code of conduct

This code applies to every public repository of Kode, to its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to anyone who speaks for Kode in them.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are writing their first firmware.
- Talk about the code, not about the person who wrote it.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you judge.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and expect them to say why.
- Write in English, in words a reader whose first language is not English can follow.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, and comments about a person's body, origin, gender, sexuality, religion, age or ability.
- Sexual language or images.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, and repeated messages after you were asked to stop.

## What happens

Write to [hello@kode.diy](mailto:hello@kode.diy) with "Conduct" in the subject. Say what happened, where, and add links or screenshots. Kode reads every report, keeps the name of the person who reports private, and answers.

A maintainer may edit or remove a comment, lock a thread, or block a person from the organisation, for a time or for good. The more serious or repeated the behaviour, the stronger the measure.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
