## What you can do without asking

- Say that your app, sketch, module or project runs on the Kode Dot, or is made for it: "a game for the Kode Dot".
- Say that your software is based on kodeOS or on the SDK: "based on kodeOS".
- Share, change and show kodeOS with Kody in it, and use the drawings and animations of Kody in your own work under their licence, as long as nobody can think that Kode made or approved it.
- Write about Kode, the Kode Dot and Kody: articles, videos, reviews and talks.

## What needs Kode's written permission

- Naming a product, a company, a firmware, an app or a repository with Kode, Kode Dot, kodeOS or Kody, or a name close to them. A firmware you build from kodeOS and distribute needs a name of its own.
- Using Kody, or a character that looks like Kody, as the logo or the mascot of your product, project or company.
- Selling anything with Kody on it: stickers, clothes, toys, cases.
- Saying or suggesting that Kode made, approved or supports your work.

## Ask

Write to [hello@kode.diy](mailto:hello@kode.diy) with "Trademark" in the subject. Say what you want to make and show it. Kode answers, and says yes more often than not.
